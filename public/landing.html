Everything in the App

Serious competition.
Questionable golf.

We take birdies very seriously.

📱
Add to Home Screen

Works like a native app on iPhone and Android. No app store — just open in Safari or Chrome and add to your home screen in seconds.

✎
Hole-by-Hole Scorecard

Enter your score per hole. Birdies, net birdies and eagles detected automatically — no manual selection needed.

🎲
Live Draw Night

Admin splits players into brackets, hits Randomise, and pairs appear live. Lock it in — the draw is permanent from that moment.

📋
Share to Messenger

One tap after logging a round generates a pre-written message. Copy and paste into the group chat. Message adapts based on how many birdies you got.

📅
Who's Playing

Post your availability with a time window and note. See who else is heading out — no more group messages asking if anyone fancies a round.

🌵
Hole Difficulty Heatmap

See which holes the group is struggling on. Red means nobody has cracked it yet. Very satisfying when it turns green.

🔥
Hot Streaks

Most birdies in a single round, who is the hot hand this week, season-long stats. The leaderboard has opinions.

📊
Net Birdie Board

Handicap-adjusted tracking runs in parallel. Stroke holes highlighted in gold. Separate leaderboard for the net competition.

Getting Started

Up and running
in four steps.

1
Sign Up

Name, password and handicap index. Get your AI nickname. Vote on the side comp. Takes 2 minutes.

2

Admin confirms your place. You are in the app and on the leaderboard.

3
Draw Night

Brackets drawn live at the pub. Head-to-head matchup locked in. Prizes confirmed on the night.

4
Play Golf

Log rounds after you play. Chase your 18 holes. Keep an eye on your bracket opponent.
